To educate, inform, and empower every consumer in Karnataka with accurate, fearless, and fair journalism rooted in the principles of natural justice.

🇮🇳 Serving Karnataka, impacting India
Meet the Editor
Dr. Vinay Kumar S.

Dr. Vinay Kumar S. is a distinguished journalist, legal mind, and consumer rights champion with decades of experience spanning journalism, banking, law, and public advocacy.

He holds a Doctorate in Journalism and brings to the role a rare combination of financial acumen (CAIIB — Chartered Associate of Indian Institute of Bankers, Mumbai) and legal training (B.Com, LLB) that sets Consumer News apart in its depth of analysis.

As Founder and Managing Trustee of the Citizen Consumer Council Trust, Dr. Vinay Kumar has directly assisted hundreds of consumers in filing complaints, seeking redressal, and understanding their rights under Indian consumer law.

He also holds leadership positions in multiple press and journalist associations, including serving as President of the Newspaper Employees Working Journalist Union and Vice President of Karnataka Patrakartra Sanghatanegala Maha Okkuta.
